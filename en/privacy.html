To review and respond to an enquiry, the controller uses information provided by the sender: an email address, phone number or messenger profile name depending on the contact channel, a name if supplied, and the message content. The form prepares a draft in the visitor's email app. The controller receives the email only after the visitor sends it. Calls, email and messenger conversations take place through the selected service. External services process data under their own policies.

Cookies and visit data

With the visitor's permission, the controller uses a web analytics service to assess website traffic and performance. YANDEX LLC processes statistical data on the controller's behalf. This includes pages visited, referrals and clicks, browser and device information, IP addresses, and identifiers in cookies or local storage. The enquiry text and enquiry consent checkbox are not sent to the analytics counter. Session recording is disabled in the website code.

The counter loads only after you select “Allow”. Your permission or refusal is stored in your browser's local storage for 180 days. You can change your choice using “Analytics settings” at the bottom of the home page. After refusal, the website stops sending statistics. The controller receives, records, stores, updates, retrieves, uses and deletes data using automated and non-automated means.
